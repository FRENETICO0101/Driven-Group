# Overview

Villa Miami is a bayfront residential experience in Edgewater, conceived around the lifestyle and hospitality of Major Food Group. The tower brings dining, wellness, service, and waterfront living into one residential setting on Biscayne Bay.

Architecture by ODP Architects is paired with interiors by Vicky Charles of Charles & Co and landscape design by Enea Garden Co. The residential experience connects life between land, water, and air, with docks at the bay and a rooftop helipad above the tower.

Residences occupy levels 6 through 52, with full-floor Villa Piano and half-floor Villa Mezzo collections. Penthouses are positioned across levels 53 through 55. The condominium is developed by 710 Edge Property, LLC; Terra and One Thousand Group are associated development teams, while Major Food Group provides the residential lifestyle and branding under license.
