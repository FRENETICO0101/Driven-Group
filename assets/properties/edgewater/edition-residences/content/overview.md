# Overview

Viceroy Brickell Residences represents a new generation of branded luxury living in Miami. Developed by Related Group and GTIS Partners, the tower combines contemporary architecture by Arquitectonica with interiors by Meyer Davis and the legendary hospitality of Viceroy Hotels & Resorts.

Positioned where the Miami River meets Brickell, the residences offer panoramic city and waterfront views while providing immediate access to Brickell City Centre, Downtown Miami, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne, and Miami International Airport.

Residents enjoy an elevated lifestyle focused on wellness, design, personalized services, and world-class amenities, making Viceroy one of the most desirable residential addresses in Brickell.
