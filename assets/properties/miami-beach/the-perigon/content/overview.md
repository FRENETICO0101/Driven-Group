# Overview

The Perigon is a private oceanfront enclave on Miami Beach, bringing 73 residences to a setting between the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay. Its cascading form is designed to draw water views and natural light into each home while preserving privacy across both horizons.

Architecture by OMA — Office for Metropolitan Architecture — is joined by interiors from Tara Bernerd & Partners and landscape design by Gustafson Porter + Bowman. Together, the design teams frame a residential experience shaped by Miami Beach's light, water, and coastal landscape.

The amenity program extends from the beach to dining, wellness, and entertaining spaces, including a residents-only oceanfront restaurant and speakeasy. Official occupancy is anticipated in Q2 2027.
