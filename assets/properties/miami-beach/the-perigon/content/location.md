## Beachfront Setting

The Perigon stands on the oceanfront at 5333 Collins Avenue, between the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay. The site meets nearly 200 feet of shoreline and is backed by the Miami Beach Boardwalk.

## Views

The residences are oriented toward both eastern Atlantic sunrises and western Biscayne Bay sunsets, with the Miami Beach cityscape between them.
