# Overview

JEM Private Residences is a luxury residential tower located within Miami Worldcenter, one of the largest mixed-use developments in the United States.

Designed to blend modern architecture with elevated hospitality-inspired living, JEM offers residents an exceptional collection of amenities, thoughtfully designed residences, and immediate access to world-class dining, shopping, entertainment, and cultural destinations.

Its strategic location places residents just minutes from Brickell, Biscayne Bay, Wynwood, the Design District, Miami International Airport, and Miami Beach, creating an ideal balance between business, leisure, and luxury living.
