# Location

Located at the entrance of Brickell, Viceroy Brickell Residences enjoys one of Miami's most desirable urban waterfront locations.

Residents are minutes away from:

- Brickell City Centre
- Miami Financial District
- Downtown Miami
- Miami River Walk
- Bayside Marketplace
- Key Biscayne
- Coconut Grove
- Design District
- Miami International Airport

The neighborhood offers a walkable lifestyle surrounded by fine dining, luxury shopping, entertainment, waterfront parks, and international business headquarters.
