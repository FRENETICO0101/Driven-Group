# Viceroy Brickell Residences

Luxury Branded Residences in the Heart of Brickell

Viceroy Brickell Residences introduces the first standalone branded residential tower by Viceroy Hotels & Resorts. Located on the Miami River at the entrance to Brickell, the project combines luxury hospitality, sophisticated design, and resort-style amenities in one of Miami's most vibrant neighborhoods.

## Highlights

- 45-story luxury residential tower
- Studios, one and two-bedroom residences
- Fully finished interiors
- Signature Viceroy hospitality services
- Extensive resort-style amenities
- Prime Brickell waterfront location
