Cipriani Residences Miami brings the legendary Italian hospitality brand to the center of Brickell.

Designed by Arquitectonica with interiors by 1508 London, the tower rises above Miami’s financial district offering expansive residences with sweeping views of Biscayne Bay, Simpson Park, and the Brickell skyline.

Inspired by nearly a century of Cipriani heritage, every residence reflects timeless design, elevated service, and effortless luxury.

Residents enjoy signature Cipriani hospitality, private dining experiences, wellness amenities, resort-style pools, and personalized concierge services.
