# Kitchens

The kitchens at St. Regis Residences Miami are designed as functional works of art, blending handcrafted Italian millwork with premium appliances and refined materials.

Highlights include:

- White Oak or White Lacquer cabinetry
- Travertine and quartz countertop selections
- Dornbracht fixtures
- Wolf cooking appliances
- Sub-Zero refrigeration
- Built-in wine storage
- Coffee systems
- Panel-ready appliances

The layouts are designed for both daily living and sophisticated entertaining.
