# Residences

The residences at St. Regis Miami offer spacious floorplans ranging from approximately 2,616 to 3,712 interior square feet.

Available layouts include:

- 2 Bedroom Residences
- 3 Bedroom Residences
- 4 Bedroom Residences

Key residential features include:

- Private elevator vestibules
- Expansive waterfront terraces
- Floor-to-ceiling windows
- Midnight bars in primary suites
- Walk-in closets
- Laundry rooms
- Service areas
- Open-concept living and dining spaces
- Biscayne Bay views
