# St. Regis Residences Miami

Located directly on Biscayne Bay in the heart of Brickell, St. Regis Residences Miami redefines luxury waterfront living through timeless design, expansive residences, and legendary St. Regis hospitality.

Created as a design-driven residential collection, the project combines refined architecture, private residential amenities, curated services, and panoramic water views to deliver one of Miami's most prestigious residential addresses.

Each residence has been conceived as a seamless extension of indoor and outdoor living, featuring oversized terraces, floor-to-ceiling glass, and private elevator access.
