# Overview

NoMad Residences Wynwood brings the first residential expression of the NoMad brand to Miami's creative district. Located at 2700 NW 2nd Avenue, the nine-story collection places art, hospitality, and flexible ownership at the center of Wynwood.

The building is developed by PRH G40 Property Owner, LLC and designed by Arquitectonica, with interiors by DesignAgency. Its 329 residences are fully furnished and curated by NoMad and DesignAgency, with layouts ranging from studios to two-bedroom homes.

Across the building, food, wellness, social spaces, and services support a residential experience shaped for everyday life and short-term rental flexibility without rental restrictions.
